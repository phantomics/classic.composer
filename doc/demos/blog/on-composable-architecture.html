On Composable Architecture Alice 2026-11-20

Building from Pieces

A composable system gives you small parts with well-defined interfaces. The whole becomes whatever you assemble. The Classic framework treats publication structure this way: blogs, forums, and wikis are different arrangements of the same primitives.

Why It Matters

Composition over configuration. When the system can express your specific case as a combination of general-purpose pieces, you stop fighting the framework. You build with it instead.
